## Open questions

* agc_vec_unit (core) loses precision on short vectors at B27: a 1 km
  LOS comes out with |u| = 1.002 (relative error 2e-3, larger than the
  input quantisation).  R63 is flown at ranges of a few km, so the core
  lane should check UNIT's pre-normalisation against INTERPRETER.agc.
* SCALEVEC assumes moon-sphere scaling (RATT1 B27); in the earth sphere
  the AEA words would be 4x too small.  This is what the AGC does (R47 is
  a lunar-vicinity routine), noted, not changed.

## Lessons

* MXV/VXM are three plain DOTs (INTERPRETER.agc p. 1144): no shift, so a
  half-unit matrix (REFSMMAT, XNB..) halves the vector.  Every scale
  computation after an MXV must count that extra B1; SCALEVEC's VSL2 /
  VSL8 only make sense with it.
* 2OCT constants whose low word has bit 15 set (AGSRND1 0000060000) are
  negative one's-complement low words; decode them before summing.
* GOMARKF's +3 return covers both ENTER and V32; test MPAC (verb) for
  BIT6 (32) as NEWAGS does, via align_resp_recycle().
* EXDSPRET continues at the cont.terminate job id (dsky.h), so a
  non-flashing display in the middle of a job needs all three ids set to
  the next segment.
* ENDEXT is ENDMARK in Luminary: it does not clear NODOFLAG; only the
  routine's own DOWNFLAG does.  Do not "tidy" flags in a shared end job.
* Keep job prototypes and provisional ids generated
  (SCRATCH/prog-align/gen.py): a hand-edited prototype drifted from its
  definition (SCALEVEC had a stray `bool moon`).
